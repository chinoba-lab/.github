# Governance

*Who is responsible for what — and how decisions are made.*

Governance is not bureaucracy bolted onto the org. It is the **direct
application of Chinoba's own research** to the project that produces it. The
existing documentation defines Governance as a theme:

> Accountable decisions need limits — boundaries, human authority, and the right
> to say no. Governance defines who is responsible for what.
> — [`../../../docs/themes/governance.md`](../../../docs/themes/governance.md)

This section makes that real for `chinoba-lab`.

## The principle, inherited

Chinoba's runtime is the only decision authority; every other layer produces
signals, follows decisions, or makes them understandable. Governance applies the
same discipline to people and repositories:

> Every change to official Chinoba is a **decision** — bounded, owned, and
> traceable. The org has clear authorities; everyone else proposes, reviews, or
> follows. There is always a human who can say **no**.

This is "boundaries, human authority, and the right to say no" expressed as an
operating model rather than a research theme.
