# Multi-Agent Systems

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Multi-Agent Systems** studies the engineering of many autonomous agents
working within one system — their interfaces, lifecycles, communication, and
failure modes. It is the practical counterpart to coordination research: the
concrete substrate on which societies and coordination protocols run.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Agent runtime** — a host for spawning and supervising agents.
- **Messaging bus** — reliable communication between agents.
- **Lifecycle manager** — starting, pausing, and retiring agents.
- **Simulation harness** — testing agent populations at scale.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Primitives** — define the agent, its interface, and its lifecycle.
2. **Communicate** — build reliable inter-agent messaging.
3. **Supervise** — handle failure and recovery.
4. **Scale** — run large populations under observation.
