# Emergent & Distributed Intelligence

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Emergent & Distributed Intelligence** studies how capability arises from the
whole rather than any single part — intelligence that is spread across many
agents and *emerges* from their interaction. It is the study of the field, not
the node.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Distribution layer** — spreading state and work across nodes.
- **Emergence probes** — detecting collective behavior.
- **Consensus tools** — agreeing without a central authority.
- **Resilience kit** — surviving partial failure gracefully.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Distribute** — spread intelligence across many nodes.
2. **Observe** — detect and describe emergent behavior.
3. **Agree** — reach consensus without a center.
4. **Endure** — stay functional under failure and churn.
