# Trust Infrastructure

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Trust Infrastructure** asks how trust is *built, measured, and maintained*
between intelligences. Rather than assuming trust, it treats trust as
infrastructure — something engineered from evidence, verifiable records, and
clear boundaries, so that cooperation can scale safely.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Trust ledger** — verifiable records of past behavior.
- **Attestation tools** — proving that a decision met its boundary.
- **Reputation model** — deriving trust scores from evidence.
- **Verification SDK** — checking claims before acting on them.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Define** — what trust means operationally in a system of agents.
2. **Evidence** — ground trust in traceable, verifiable records.
3. **Measure** — turn evidence into usable trust signals.
4. **Maintain** — keep trust current as behavior changes.
