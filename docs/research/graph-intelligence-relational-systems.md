# Graph Intelligence & Relational Systems

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Graph Intelligence & Relational Systems** studies intelligence expressed
through relationships — nodes, edges, and the patterns between them. It treats
the graph as the natural shape of knowledge and decision, where meaning lives in
connection rather than in isolated points.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Graph store** — a home for relational data at scale.
- **Relational learning** — models that reason over structure.
- **Traversal engine** — querying paths and neighborhoods.
- **Graph-of-traces** — connecting decision traces relationally.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Model** — represent knowledge and decisions as graphs.
2. **Learn** — build models that operate on relational structure.
3. **Query** — traverse and reason over the graph.
4. **Connect** — link traces, knowledge, and agents into one graph.
