# Physical AI & Cyber-Physical Systems

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Physical AI & Cyber-Physical Systems** studies intelligence that acts in the
physical world — robots, sensors, and actuators where decisions have material,
irreversible consequences. It brings the decision-trace discipline to systems
where the boundary between computation and reality is thin.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Sensor abstraction** — a uniform interface to physical signals.
- **Actuation guard** — bounding physical actions safely.
- **Real-time runtime** — decisions under hard timing constraints.
- **Digital twin** — simulating the physical system for testing.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Sense** — turn physical signals into decision inputs.
2. **Act** — bound physical actions with safety boundaries.
3. **Time** — meet real-time constraints reliably.
4. **Mirror** — validate against simulation before deployment.
