# Knowledge Flow

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Knowledge Flow** studies how knowledge moves — between agents, across time, and
through decisions. Knowledge is treated not as a static store but as something
*in motion*: produced, transformed, and consumed as it travels through a system
of intelligences.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Knowledge graph core** — a moving, versioned representation of what is known.
- **Flow protocol** — how knowledge is published and subscribed to.
- **Provenance layer** — tracking where each piece of knowledge came from.
- **Distillation tools** — compressing flow into reusable knowledge.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Represent** — settle on how a unit of knowledge is captured.
2. **Move** — define the channels knowledge travels through.
3. **Trace** — attach provenance so flow stays accountable.
4. **Reuse** — turn accumulated flow into durable knowledge.
