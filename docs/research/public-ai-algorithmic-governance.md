# Public AI & Algorithmic Governance

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Public AI & Algorithmic Governance** studies intelligence that serves the
public and the algorithms that govern shared life. It asks how automated
decisions can remain legitimate, contestable, and accountable when they affect
communities rather than individual users.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Public trace registry** — inspectable records of public decisions.
- **Contestation protocol** — a way to challenge an algorithmic decision.
- **Transparency reports** — turning traces into public accountability.
- **Impact assessment** — evaluating effects before deployment.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Expose** — make public decisions inspectable.
2. **Contest** — give people a path to challenge them.
3. **Assess** — evaluate community-level impact.
4. **Legitimize** — ground public AI in accountable process.
