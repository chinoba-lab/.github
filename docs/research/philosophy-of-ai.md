# Philosophy of AI

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Philosophy of AI** examines the questions beneath the engineering — what
intelligence *is*, what it means for a decision to be accountable, and why
Chinoba treats intelligence as relational rather than individual. It is the
source the rest of the research reasons from.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Concept lexicon** — a shared vocabulary for the ecosystem.
- **Principle references** — machine- and human-readable statements of values.
- **Essay collection** — long-form reasoning behind design choices.
- **Alignment notes** — connecting principles to implementation.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Articulate** — state the core philosophical commitments.
2. **Define** — build a precise, shared vocabulary.
3. **Connect** — trace each principle into the technology.
4. **Revisit** — refine the philosophy as the work matures.
