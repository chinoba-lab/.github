# Runtime Society

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Runtime Society** studies what happens when many decision-making runtimes
coexist and interact. Instead of a single model producing answers, a *society*
of runtimes negotiates, defers, and coordinates — and the health of that society
becomes as important as the intelligence of any one member.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Society runtime** — a host for many cooperating decision runtimes.
- **Membership protocol** — how a runtime joins, leaves, and is trusted.
- **Interaction log** — a shared record of inter-runtime exchanges.
- **Health metrics** — observability for the society as a whole.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Model** — describe a runtime society as a first-class structure.
2. **Coordinate** — define the protocols that keep members aligned.
3. **Observe** — measure the emergent behavior of the whole.
4. **Govern** — connect society health to boundary and trust systems.
