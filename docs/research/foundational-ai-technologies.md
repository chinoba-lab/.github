## Overview

**Foundational AI Technologies** studies the core primitives every higher-level
system depends on — representation, learning, inference, and memory. It is the
shared toolbox from which decision runtimes, agents, and knowledge systems are
built.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Representation library** — shared encodings for signals and state.
- **Inference core** — a reusable engine for drawing conclusions.
- **Memory substrate** — durable state across decisions.
- **Learning toolkit** — updating models from experience.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Represent** — stabilize the core data representations.
2. **Infer** — provide a shared inference engine.
3. **Remember** — give systems durable memory.
4. **Learn** — close the loop with experience-driven updates.
