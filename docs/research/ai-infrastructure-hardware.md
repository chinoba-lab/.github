# AI Infrastructure & Hardware

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**AI Infrastructure & Hardware** studies the physical and systems foundation
that intelligence runs on — compute, memory, networking, and accelerators. It
asks how the shape of the hardware constrains and enables the decisions built on
top of it.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Resource scheduler** — allocating compute across workloads.
- **Runtime profiler** — measuring cost and latency of decisions.
- **Accelerator abstraction** — a portable layer over hardware.
- **Deployment toolkit** — shipping runtimes to real infrastructure.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Abstract** — a portable interface over heterogeneous hardware.
2. **Schedule** — allocate resources fairly and efficiently.
3. **Measure** — profile cost, latency, and throughput.
4. **Deploy** — move runtimes to production reliably.
