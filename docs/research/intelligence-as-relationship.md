# Intelligence as Relationship

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Intelligence as Relationship** is the thesis at the center of everything
Chinoba does: intelligence is not a property of an isolated mind but something
that lives *between* — between signal and decision, between agents, between a
system and the people who depend on it. It is the ∞ that ties every other
research area together.
