# AI Economics & Intelligence Field Economics

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**AI Economics & Intelligence Field Economics** studies how value, cost, and
incentive flow through systems of intelligences. It asks what it means to
allocate scarce attention, compute, and trust — and how an *intelligence field*
behaves as an economy where decisions carry price and consequence.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Cost model** — accounting for compute, attention, and trust.
- **Incentive engine** — aligning agent behavior with system goals.
- **Market simulator** — modeling exchange between intelligences.
- **Field metrics** — measuring value flow across the field.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Account** — define the units of value in an intelligence field.
2. **Incentivize** — connect rewards to desirable behavior.
3. **Exchange** — model markets and allocation between agents.
4. **Balance** — study stability and fairness of the field economy.
