# AI Coordination

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**AI Coordination** studies how independent intelligences act *together* toward
shared outcomes — how they align, divide work, resolve conflict, and stay
coherent without a single central controller. Coordination is treated as a
relationship to be designed, not a side effect to be hoped for.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Coordination protocol** — a shared language for aligning actions.
- **Orchestrator core** — assigning and sequencing work across agents.
- **Conflict resolution** — deciding when agents disagree.
- **Alignment metrics** — measuring how coherent a group stays.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Protocol** — define how agents signal intent to each other.
2. **Orchestrate** — build the machinery that routes work.
3. **Resolve** — handle disagreement and contention gracefully.
4. **Scale** — keep coordination stable as the group grows.
