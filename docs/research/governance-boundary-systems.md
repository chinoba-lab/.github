# Governance & Boundary Systems

← [Back to Research Hub](README.md)

## Overview

**Governance & Boundary Systems** studies the limits within which intelligence
is allowed to act. A boundary is where a decision meets its constraints —
policy, safety, permission — and governance is the machinery that defines,
enforces, and evolves those boundaries over time.

## Future Implementations

Planned OSS components appropriate to this topic:

- **Policy engine** — expressing and evaluating boundaries.
- **Boundary guard** — intercepting actions that cross a limit.
- **Governance ledger** — recording how boundaries change.
- **Override protocol** — safe human intervention at the edge.

---

## Research Roadmap

1. **Express** — define boundaries in a checkable form.
2. **Enforce** — evaluate every action against them.
3. **Evolve** — govern how boundaries themselves change.
4. **Account** — log boundary events for review.
