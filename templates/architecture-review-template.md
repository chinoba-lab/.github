## Proposal

What design is being proposed? Summarize it in the reviewer's own words to
confirm understanding.

## Motivation

Why is this design needed? What does it make possible?

## Alternatives

What other designs were considered? Why is this one on the table?

## Advantages

What the design does well.

## Risks

Where it could fail, what it costs, and what it couples together.

## Feedback

Concrete, actionable comments. What should change, and why?

## Decision

Accept, revise, or decline — **with a reason**. A decline is a valid, valuable
outcome; record it as carefully as an acceptance.

## Future Improvements

What to revisit later. If accepted, this becomes the seed of the next
[Architecture Decision Record](../docs/architecture/adr/).
