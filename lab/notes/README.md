# Research Notes

*An idea written down and reasoned through.*

---

## What this is

A research note is the moment an idea stops being a conversation and becomes a
*written argument*. It takes a question from [discussions](../discussions/) and
works it through: what is being asked, what is already known, what the insight
is, and what remains open.

Notes are living documents. They carry a **Status**, they are revised as the
idea develops, and they link back to the discussions that shaped them and
forward to the proposals and experiments they inspire.

> Knowledge evolves through discussion. A research note is where that evolution
> is recorded.

---

## What a note contains

Notes follow [`../../templates/research-note-template.md`](../../templates/research-note-template.md):

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What the note is about |
| **Research Question** | The question the note is trying to answer |
| **Background** | What is already known, and why the question matters |
| **Discussion** | The reasoning — worked through in the open |
| **Insight** | What was learned or proposed |
| **Related Work** | How this connects to other ideas, inside and outside Chinoba |
| **References** | Sources |
| **Future Work** | What remains open |
| **Status** | Where the note is on its path |

---

## Status values

A note is never simply "done." Its status says where it is:

- **Draft** — being written; expect it to change.
- **In Discussion** — open for feedback.
- **Under Review** — being examined against the standards.
- **Stable** — settled enough to rely on.
- **Promoted** — moved into the [Library](../../docs/research/).

---

## From note to Library

When a note settles, it can be **promoted** into the research Library at
[`../../docs/research/`](../../docs/research/). The note itself stays here as the
record of how the idea developed — the Library holds the conclusion, the
Laboratory keeps its history.
