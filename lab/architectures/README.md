## What this is

This room holds **architectures under development** — designs that have grown out
of a [proposal](../proposals/) and are being shaped, challenged, and revised in
public. It is the Laboratory counterpart to the stable architecture in the
Library at [`../../docs/architecture/`](../../docs/architecture/).

> Architecture evolves through review. What lives here is not the final design —
> it is the design *while it is still being decided*.

An architecture in this room is expected to change. Its revisions are part of
its record: you should be able to read not only the current design but the path
it took to get there.

---

## Architecture and review

An architecture and its [reviews](../reviews/) are two halves of one dialogue:

- The **architecture** states the design.
- The **review** questions it — motivation, alternatives, advantages, risks.
- The design is **revised** in response, and the cycle repeats.

Use [`../../templates/architecture-review-template.md`](../../templates/architecture-review-template.md)
to review a design here.

---

## From here to the Library

When an architecture settles, its decision is captured as an **Architecture
Decision Record** in the Library
([`../../docs/architecture/adr/`](../../docs/architecture/adr/)), and the stable
design is described in [`../../docs/architecture/`](../../docs/architecture/).

The working design stays here as the record of how the decision was reached.

```
Proposal → Architecture (here) → Review → Revision → ADR (Library)
```
