## What this is

This room holds **talks and lectures** — the stage where knowledge is prepared to
be *communicated* to an audience. It is the far end of the research path: an idea
that began as an open question, matured through discussion and experiment, and is
now clear enough to teach.

> Communication creates Intelligence. A lecture is not the end of research — it
> is research returning to the dialogue it came from, now able to start new ones
> in the people who hear it.

---

## What lives here

- **Talk outlines and slides** — the structure of a spoken argument.
- **Lecture notes** — longer-form material for teaching.
- **Recordings and links** — where a talk has been given publicly.
- **Follow-up questions** — what the audience raised, which often becomes the
  next [discussion](../discussions/).

---

## Talks close the loop

A lecture is where the research path curves back to its beginning. The questions
an audience asks are new signals; the best of them return to
[`../discussions/`](../discussions/) and begin the workflow again.

```
Idea → … → Paper → Book → Public Lecture → new Questions → Discussion
```

This is the laboratory's most direct expression of its philosophy: knowledge
shared aloud creates more knowledge in return.
