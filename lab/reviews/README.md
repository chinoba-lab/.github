# Reviews

*The dialogue that refines a design.*

## What this is

This room holds **reviews** — of [architectures](../architectures/),
[proposals](../proposals/), and [papers](../papers/). A review is not a verdict
handed down from above; it is a considered response that helps an idea become its
best version.

> Ideas mature through collaboration. A review is that collaboration written
> down.

Reviews are public. The value of Chinoba Lab is not that its designs are correct
on the first try, but that the dialogue which corrects them is visible.

---

## What a review does

A good review is honest about both sides of a design. Following
[`../../templates/architecture-review-template.md`](../../templates/architecture-review-template.md),
a review examines:

| Element | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Proposal** | What is being proposed? |
| **Motivation** | Why does it matter? |
| **Alternatives** | What else was considered? |
| **Advantages** | What does it do well? |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong? |
| **Feedback** | What should change? |
| **Decision** | Accept, revise, or decline — with a reason |
| **Future Improvements** | What to revisit later |

---

## Review as a Chinoba decision

A review is the organization's **Signal → Decision** loop applied to research:

- the proposal or architecture is the **signal**;
- the standards are the **boundary**;
- the reviewer is the **human gate**;
- the decision and its reason are the **trace**.

→ How decisions are recorded across Chinoba:
[`../../docs/governance/decision-rights.md`](../../docs/governance/decision-rights.md)

A decision to **decline** is as valuable as a decision to accept — both are
recorded, both keep the Commons trustworthy.
