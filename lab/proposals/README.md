## What this is

A proposal is an idea made specific enough to act on. Where a
[research note](../notes/) reasons through a question, a proposal says: *here is
what we should build, adopt, or change — and why.*

A proposal is written to be reviewed. It states its motivation, considers the
alternatives, and names its risks honestly, so that the people reviewing it can
make an accountable decision.

> A proposal is a signal. A review is the dialogue. The decision — and its
> reason — is the trace.

---

## What a proposal contains

Proposals follow [`../../templates/proposal-template.md`](../../templates/proposal-template.md).
A good proposal makes it easy to say yes *or* no with confidence: it is explicit
about what it is asking for, what it would cost, and what could go wrong.

---

## Proposals and architecture

A proposal about a *design* becomes an **architecture proposal**. Once written,
it moves into [architecture review](../reviews/), where it is challenged and
revised, and its evolving design lives in [`../architectures/`](../architectures/).

This mirrors the existing **Architecture Decision Records** in the Library
([`../../docs/architecture/adr/`](../../docs/architecture/adr/)): an ADR is a
proposal that has been decided. A proposal here is an ADR before its decision —
still open, still in dialogue.

---

## The path of a proposal

```
Research Note → Proposal → Review → Decision → (Experiment or Architecture) → Revision
```

A proposal can be accepted, declined, or sent back for revision. Every outcome
is recorded with its reason. Declining a proposal is not a failure of the
process; it is the process working.
