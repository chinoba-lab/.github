## What this is

This is the first room of the Laboratory. Before an idea becomes a note, a
proposal, or an architecture, it is a **question** — and questions belong here,
in the open, where they can be shaped by more than one mind.

> Communication creates Intelligence. A discussion is not preparation for the
> research; it is the research beginning.

Discussions are deliberately unfinished. They are allowed to be speculative,
wrong, and revised. Their value is the dialogue, not the conclusion.

---

## Kinds of discussion

| Kind | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Open Questions** | Questions with no settled answer yet — the raw material of the lab |
| **Architecture Discussion** | Debating how something should be designed, before a proposal exists |
| **Paper Discussion** | Working through the argument, framing, or claims of a paper |
| **Book Discussion** | Shaping longer-form knowledge as it is assembled |
| **University Discussion** | Dialogue with academic researchers and institutions |
| **Industry Discussion** | Dialogue with companies and practitioners |
| **Research Collaboration** | Coordinating joint work across groups |
| **Future Ideas** | Speculative directions the lab might one day take |

---

## Where a discussion lives

A discussion can live in two places, and they serve different purposes:

- **GitHub Discussions** — for the live, threaded conversation. This is where
  the back-and-forth happens.
- **This directory** — for a durable *record* of a discussion worth keeping: its
  question, the positions taken, and where it led.

## How to open one

1. Start from [`../../templates/discussion-template.md`](../../templates/discussion-template.md).
2. State the **question** clearly — the question matters more than any early answer.
3. Link to related notes, proposals, or research areas.
4. Invite feedback. Leave it open.

When a discussion produces something worth developing, it moves forward — into a
[research note](../notes/), a [proposal](../proposals/), or an
[experiment](../experiments/).
