# Chinoba Lab

**Intelligence as Relationship**

The official open-source organization for **Chinoba** — an open research
ecosystem building the infrastructure for Human–AI Coordination.

[chinoba.org](https://chinoba.org)

## What is Chinoba

Chinoba is built on a single idea: **intelligence is not a property of a model —
it emerges in relationship.** It lives in how AI, humans, organizations, and
society trust each other, share what they know, decide, and act together.

So the work here is not better models. It is the **infrastructure of the
relationship** — trust, knowledge, accountable decisions, and the coordination
that lets humans and AI act together in the open.

> **AI is not prediction. It is decision.**

---

## What lives here

`chinoba-lab` hosts the **official, open-source implementation** of Chinoba,
organized into five layers around a single principle: the runtime is the only
decision authority.

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Interaction** | Turns messages and evidence into structured signals |
| **Core / Decision Runtime** | The single point of decision — evaluates, bounds, records |
| **Execution** | Enacts finalized decisions across agents, tools, and systems |
| **Learning** | Learns from ledgered traces and emits better signals |
| **Design** | Designs, simulates, and improves decision systems |

> Every layer produces signals, follows decisions, or makes them
> understandable. Only the runtime decides.

→ The full architecture and decision flow lives at [chinoba.org](https://chinoba.org).

## The relationship to the founder's account

Chinoba began — and continues to experiment — on the founder's personal account,
[`Masao-Watanabe-AI`](https://github.com/masao-watanabe-ai). That account is the
**Bench**: experiments, prototypes, and the archive of earlier work. This
organization is the **Commons**: the official, supported ecosystem. Ideas
graduate from one to the other.
